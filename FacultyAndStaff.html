Academic Affairs

    Dr. Odalys Ferran
  • — Applied Folklore Spent a decade collecting oral histories in the Appalachian foothills before joining Wrenfield. Holds office hours in the campus greenhouse "because stories grow better around plants." Her seminar on urban legend transmission is standing-room-only every spring.

    • Prof. Micah Sato
  • — Cartography & Speculative Geography Former merchant-marine navigator turned professor. Teaches the notoriously difficult "Mapping the Unmappable" seminar, where the final project is a fully-defended map of somewhere that doesn't exist. Keeps a sextant on his desk that he insists still works.

    • Dr. Beatrice Holt
  • — Dendrochronology & Memory Studies Runs the Alder Hollow field station and is single-handedly responsible for the college's tree-ring archive, which she describes as "the world's slowest library." Famously hard to reach by email; reachable by knocking on the field station door.

    • Prof. Elias Kwan
  • — Silviculture & Forest Stewardship Came to teaching after twenty years with the county forestry service. Runs controlled burns as part of his course and has a standing rule that any student who falls asleep in his 8 a.m. lecture has to help carry equipment on the next field day.

Welcome to Wrenfield College

- A small liberal-arts college in the town of Wrenfield, founded in 1889, known for traditions and a strong arts/science balance.

Where curious minds map the uncharted.

Est. 1887 · Wrenfield, [State]

] Wrenfield College is a small liberal arts community built on an old idea: that the best learning happens at the edges of disciplines. Here, cartographers study speculative geography, musicians record the sounds of the county's rivers, and folklorists chase stories through the fog that rolls over the quad every fall. Whatever you're drawn to, you'll find room here to follow it further than you expected. [Explore Programs]
